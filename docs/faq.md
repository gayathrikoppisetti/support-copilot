# Taskly Frequently Asked Questions

## What does Taskly cost?
Taskly has three plans. Free supports up to 5 users and 3 projects. Pro costs $10 per user per month, or $8 per user per month when billed annually. Business costs $15 per user per month and adds SSO, audit logs, and priority support.

## Can I get a refund?
Yes. If you buy an annual plan and change your mind, you can request a refund within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the billing period.

## How do I invite a teammate?
Go to Settings, then Members, then click Invite. Enter their email address and choose a role (Admin, Member, or Guest). They will receive an email with a link to join your workspace.

## Can I export my data?
Yes. Go to Settings, then Data, then Export. You can download all tasks and projects as CSV or JSON. Exports are emailed to the workspace owner within a few minutes.

## Does Taskly work offline?
The mobile apps support offline mode. Changes you make offline sync automatically when you reconnect.
