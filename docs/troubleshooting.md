# Troubleshooting Guide

## Error E-4012: Session expired
This error means your login session is no longer valid. Fix it by clearing your browser cookies for taskly.example, closing the tab, and signing in again. If your company uses SSO, sign out of your identity provider too, then retry. If the error keeps coming back, check that your computer's clock is set correctly, because a wrong system time can invalidate sessions.

## Invite email not received
If a teammate says they never received your invitation, first ask them to check their spam or junk folder. Then go to Settings, then Members, find the pending invite, and click Resend. Remember that invitation links expire after 7 days. If the link has expired, send a new invite. Some corporate email filters block messages from new senders, so ask their IT team to allow mail from taskly.example.

## Mobile app not syncing
Pull down on the task list to force a refresh. Make sure the app is updated to the latest version and that offline mode is turned off in Settings. If tasks still do not appear, sign out and back in. On Android, also check that battery saver is not restricting background data for Taskly.

## Notifications not arriving
Check that notifications are enabled both inside Taskly (Settings, then Notifications) and in your phone's system settings. Also confirm you are not in Do Not Disturb mode.
