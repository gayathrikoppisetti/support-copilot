# Getting Started with Taskly

## Step 1: Create your workspace
After signing up, name your workspace after your team or company. The workspace is the top-level container for all your projects and members.

## Step 2: Create your first project
Click New Project, give it a name, and choose a template: Kanban board, List, or Timeline. You can change the view later without losing any data.

## Step 3: Add tasks
Inside a project, click Add Task. Each task can have an assignee, a due date, labels, and subtasks. Use the comment thread on a task to discuss it with your team.

## Step 4: Invite your teammates
Open Settings, then Members, then Invite. Enter email addresses and pick a role. Admins can manage billing and members, Members can edit tasks, and Guests can only view and comment. Invitation links stay valid for 7 days, so remind people to accept them promptly.

## Step 5: Connect your tools
Under Settings, then Integrations, you can connect Slack, Google Calendar, and GitHub. Slack sends task updates into a channel of your choice. For custom integrations, see our API documentation.

## Tips
Start with one project and a small team. Add complexity only when you need it.
