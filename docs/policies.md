# Taskly Policies

## Refund policy
Annual plan purchases can be refunded in full within 30 days of the purchase date. Monthly plans are not eligible for refunds. To request a refund, contact support from the workspace owner's email address. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Pricing summary
The Pro plan is $10 per user per month on monthly billing, or $96 per user per year on annual billing. The Business plan is $15 per user per month. Prices exclude applicable taxes.

## Account deletion and data retention
When you delete a workspace, all its data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently purged. Deleted data cannot be recovered after the 30-day period. Backups are retained for an additional 14 days for disaster recovery only.

## Service level agreement
Business plan customers are covered by a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment. If uptime falls below this, affected customers receive service credits. The SLA does not apply to Free or Pro plans.

## Acceptable use
Accounts used for spam, harassment, or illegal content may be suspended without refund.
